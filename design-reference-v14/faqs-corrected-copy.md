# Corrected FAQ copy for fantasymmadness.com/faqs

Two live answers currently contradict the confirmed scoring rules. Replace both with the text below.

---

## FAQ #1 — "What if there is a knockout (KO)?"

**Current (wrong):**
> Even if the fight ends in a knockout, the fight will still be fully scored as if it went the distance (with no KO). This ensures that the scoring remains consistent, even if the fight ends quickly, and prevents the game from feeling too skewed toward those who just happen to pick a knockout.

**Corrected:**
> A knockout, TKO, or submission is a Finish — and correctly calling the round it happens in pays a **500-point Finish Bonus** on top of your round-winner points. If you pick the right fighter to win the finish round, you earn 100 (Round Winner) + 25 (auto-paired) + 500 (Finish Bonus) = **625 points** for that round alone. If you miss the finish round pick, that round scores 0. Every other round pays a flat 25-point Survival Bonus regardless of accuracy, plus your round-winner points if you called it right.

## FAQ — the stat-accuracy answer (currently under "How do I score the fight?" / the punches example)

**Current (wrong):**
> Points are awarded based on accurate predictions, such as head punches, body punches, round winners, and knockouts. Overestimations are not penalized, but only accurate or lower-than-actual predictions earn points. For example, predicting fewer punches than thrown will still earn points, while predicting more does not.

**Corrected:**
> Your stat predictions (punches, strikes, kicks, and more) work like a floor you're trying to clear. If the fighter's actual number is **equal to or higher** than your prediction, you score — and you earn points equal to exactly what you predicted. Predict 25 punches and he throws 25 or more? You get 25 points. Predict 100 and he only throws 40? You get 0 — you guessed too high. The higher you accurately predict, the more you win, so studying the matchup pays off far more than playing it safe with a low guess.
