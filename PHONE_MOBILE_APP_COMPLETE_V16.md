# Fantasy MMAdness Mobile App Complete Build V16

## Scope
- Rebuilt the supplied mobile-app handoff natively inside the existing Next.js frontend.
- The experience is isolated to phone breakpoints; the desktop homepage remains unchanged.

## Implemented mobile screens
1. Home
2. Contests
3. Make Predictions
4. Leaderboard and Hall of Fame
5. Leagues, Fantasy Cards, public leagues, and challenges
6. Watch Party with live clock, reactions, and Cage Cam chat
7. Profile and Fight IQ receipt
8. Settings, with staff-only tools gated to admin roles
9. Free Demo Walkthrough
10. Blogs and Fight News
11. Complete menu drawer and fixed app navigation

## Data and interactions
- Uses existing fight, fighter, poster, countdown, prize, entry, prediction, wallet, level, and leaderboard data.
- Sport-specific scorecards follow the handoff scoring terminology.
- Pro Wrestling uses Match Time Range options instead of rounds.
- Includes app sound toggle, tap feedback, live animations, carousels, reward interactions, and safe image fallbacks.

## Assets
- Added the supplied arena, friends-watching, ring-corner, chest, and affiliate images under `public/images/mobile-app-v16/`.
